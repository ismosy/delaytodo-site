2. Overview

DelayTodo is a simple reminder application that allows users to create and manage personal reminders on their device. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed and how your privacy is protected.

3. Data We Collect

DelayTodo is designed to minimize data collection.

  • Reminder data: text content and scheduled times you create within the app
  • Settings: preferences such as notification settings, time format, and delay options
  • Imported or exported files: only when you choose to import from or export to a file

All data is stored locally on your device. The developer does not have access to your reminder data or settings.

4. Notifications

The app uses local notifications to remind you of scheduled items. Notification data is processed entirely on your device and is not transmitted externally.

5. Analytics and Tracking

DelayTodo does not use analytics, tracking, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking technologies.

6. Data Storage and Security

Reminder data and settings are stored locally on your device. No reminder content is transmitted to external servers.

7. Optional Future Features

Future versions of the app may include optional synchronization features (such as cloud backup or multi-device sync).

If such features are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to clearly describe what data is transmitted and how it is handled.

8. Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app will remove all associated data stored locally.

9. Your Rights

As no personal data is collected or processed externally, most data protection rights can be exercised directly by managing or deleting data within the app or by uninstalling it.

If you have questions, you may contact the developer using the details below.

10. Children

DelayTodo is not specifically directed at children, but it does not collect personal data from users of any age.
