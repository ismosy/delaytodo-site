## Description (max 4000 characters)

DelayTodo is a fast reminder app for the moments when life does not follow the plan.

Create a reminder, get notified when it is due, and reschedule it in one tap. No accounts. No subscriptions. Everything stays on your device.

**Quick-select rescheduling**
When a reminder fires, a dedicated reschedule screen appears. Large buttons let you delay to the most common times: Hush (next half-hour), a quick delay, Afternoon, Evening, Tomorrow, or Next Week. You can also set a precise custom time with the scroll wheel. The quick buttons are configurable in Settings.

**Stay on top of what matters**
Reminders can alert you with sound and vibration even when the phone is locked or when you are using another app. Tap Hush from the notification itself to snooze to the next half-hour without opening the app. Tap Dismiss to clear it for good.

**Group scheduling**
Schedule multiple reminders for the same time and manage them together. Edit the time for an entire group at once. The app organises related reminders automatically — no manual tagging needed.

**Fully configurable**
- Choose your own times for Afternoon, Evening, Tomorrow, and Next Week quick buttons
- 12-hour or 24-hour clock (or follow the system setting)
- Alarm sound on/off (or choose system alarm tone on Android)
- Vibration on/off
- App sounds and haptics on/off
- Week starts on Monday or Sunday

**Simple and clean**
Dark and light modes follow your system setting automatically. No accounts. No subscriptions. No data leaves your device.

**Export and import**
Back up your reminders to a JSON file or move them to a new device in seconds. Import restores everything with the original timestamps.

## App Review Notes (for App Review team)

DelayTodo is an offline reminder app with no backend, accounts, analytics, or advertising.

To test:
1. Create a reminder a few minutes in the future
2. Wait for the notification to fire
3. Tap the notification to open the reschedule screen
4. Use the quick-select buttons or the time wheel to reschedule
5. Use the Hush notification action from the lock screen to snooze without opening the app

Notification permission is requested on first launch.
